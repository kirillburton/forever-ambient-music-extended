# Art

- `fame-logo.svg`: the logo, vector source.
- `fame-logo-512.png`: CurseForge avatar and README image.
- `../ForeverAmbientMusicExtended/Media/logo.tga`: 128 px, the addon list icon and the minimap button.
- `../ForeverAmbientMusicExtended/Media/portrait.tga`: 128 px, the music window's round portrait.
  The logo rendered at 372 px on a 512 px dark disc (11, 38, 46), shifted 9 px left
  and 12 px up from centre, then scaled to 128. The offset was measured in game
  against Forever's portrait ring, which isn't centred on the texture: walking
  out from the badge's centre, the dark gap to the ring should be equal on all
  four sides (it was 9 / 9 left-right and 9 / 1 top-bottom before the last 10 px).
